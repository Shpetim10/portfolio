GOAL
The manifesto text and the sculpture form one moment: as the words light up, Victory
assembles, and her wings finish spreading as the final manifesto sentence lights up.
The portrait moves from the right column into a signature block that closes the manifesto.

DESKTOP (≥1024px)
- Pin the section for about 180vh. Manifesto text in columns 1–7; the sculpture
  (desktop grid) in columns 8–12, vertically centered, height min(80vh, 780px),
  never cropped. She faces left, toward the text.
- One ScrollTrigger progress value p (0→1):
  · word highlight (existing dust → bone effect) maps to p 0 → 0.88;
  · sculpture progress maps so that it reaches 1 exactly when the last word of the
    final sentence lights up (compute from the word index, not a fixed number);
  · at p ≥ 0.9: ANNOTATE motion draws a leader line from the leading wing edge to a
    PartLabel at the lower right: line 1 "MATERIAL: CODE", line 2 "DIRECTION: FORWARD".
- Figcaption under the sculpture, micro mono, dust:
  "After the Winged Victory of Samothrace, c. 190 BC. Rebuilt in code."
- No extra headline in this section; the manifesto remains the only large text.
- Keep at least 40vh of unpinned scroll between the hero's pinned scene and this pin;
  refresh ScrollTrigger in the correct order.

TABLET AND MOBILE (<1024px)
- No pinning. The sculpture (mobile grid) sits in a sticky area at the top of the
  section: height 46svh, carbon background, with a 32px carbon gradient fade at its
  bottom edge so the text slides beneath it cleanly.
- The manifesto text scrolls below. Progress p runs from the section top reaching the
  viewport top until the last manifesto line passes the middle of the screen; same
  mapping as desktop. The annotation appears as a static label under the sculpture
  at p ≥ 0.9.

SIGNATURE BLOCK (all sizes, after the manifesto, inside the same section)
- A hairline rule, then the portrait (existing asset, 4:5; desktop 220px wide in
  columns 1–3; mobile full width up to 360px), with the name in heading size,
  "Software Engineer · Tirana" in body, and a hairline signature line with
  "— Shpëtim Shabanaj" in the text font.
- Keep the portrait's clip-path reveal and alt text. Remove the old right-column
  portrait, its dimension line and its part label.

REDUCED MOTION
- No pin, no sticky. Sculpture in its final server-rendered state, manifesto fully
  bone, annotation visible, portrait fades in over 200 ms.

ACCEPT
- Manifesto stays real, selectable text in correct reading order; readable with JS off.
- The wings complete on the final sentence when scrolling at any speed, both ways.
- No layout shift when the canvas replaces the pre; no horizontal scroll at 320px;
  the sticky area never exceeds 46svh.
- Mobile Lighthouse performance drops by no more than 3 points versus before.
- Report: files changed; screenshots at 375, 768 and 1440 at p = 0, 0.5 and 1;

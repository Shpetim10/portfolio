TASK T09d · Proportion bridge between Experience and Education · Roles: UI + Motion
Read: AGENTS.md, docs/design-system.md
Depends on: T09c (GlyphLineDrawing) and T09b (Education) merged.

GOAL
Insert a short bridge band between the Experience section and the Education section.
It shows the Vitruvian Man drawing itself as it scrolls past. The square stands for
practice (Experience, above) and the circle for theory (Education, below). The band
must feel like a connection between the two sections, not a separate section.
Do NOT pin it.

STRUCTURE
- New component src/components/sections/ProportionBridge.tsx, placed between
  Experience and Education on the homepage. It is a <figure> inside an
  <aside aria-label="Proportion">, not a numbered section; do not renumber
  section indexes. The fixed section indicator shows "PROPORTION" (no number)
  while the band is in view.
- Reduce Experience's bottom padding and Education's top padding to half the normal
  section padding, so the three read as one continuous passage.
- A 1px dust hairline runs vertically from the end of the Experience section down to
  the top center of the square, and from the bottom center of the circle down to the
  start of the Education section, using the anchors from onLayout. The sections are
  physically connected through the drawing.

DESKTOP (≥1024px)
- Band height about 110vh. The drawing sits centered in columns 4–9; its square is
  min(64vh, 620px) wide.
- Progress p = scroll progress from when the band's top reaches 85% of the viewport
  height until its bottom reaches 35%. Pass p straight to GlyphLineDrawing.
- Labels, using the existing LeaderLine + PartLabel components and the ANNOTATE motion:
  · when p ≥ 0.30 (square complete): a leader line from squareTopRight going up and
    right to a label in columns 10–12: line 1 "PRACTICE", line 2 "↑ EXPERIENCE".
  · when p ≥ 0.55 (circle complete): a leader line from circleBottomLeft going down
    and left to a label in columns 1–3: line 1 "THEORY", line 2 "↓ EDUCATION".
  · Line 2 of each label is a real link to #experience and #education respectively,
    with visible focus and smooth scroll (Lenis).
- Figcaption under the drawing, right-aligned, micro mono, dust:
  "After Leonardo da Vinci, Vitruvian Man, c. 1490." Add the scan's credit line if
  its license requires one.
- No headline, no slogan. The two labels carry the meaning.

TABLET AND MOBILE (<1024px)
- Band height about 90svh. The drawing is centered, full width minus the margins,
  max 92vw.
- "PRACTICE / ↑ EXPERIENCE" sits centered above the drawing, connected to the square's
  top center by a short vertical leader line; "THEORY / ↓ EDUCATION" sits centered
  below it, connected to the circle's bottom center. Both appear at the same p values.
- Same progress mapping; no pinning, no sticky.

REDUCED MOTION
- Final drawing state, both labels visible, connecting hairlines visible, no animation.

ACCEPT
- No pinning anywhere in the band; scrolling never pauses or slows here.
- Labels stay attached to the square and circle at every width from 320px to 2560px.
- The links scroll to the correct sections and are reachable by keyboard.
- The band adds no layout shift and keeps mobile Lighthouse performance within
  2 points of before.
- Section indexes, menu and sitemap order are unchanged; only the indicator gains
  the "PROPORTION" state.
- Report: files changed; screenshots at 375, 768 and 1440 at p = 0.3, 0.6 and 1;
  a reduced-motion screenshot.

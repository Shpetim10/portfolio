<Chapter id="problem">

Libraries that track books, stock and orders by hand lose time and make mistakes, and readers have no easy way to browse or order online.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="constraints">

A course timeline, a student team and a classic web stack without frameworks: HTML, SCSS and JavaScript on the frontend, PHP and MySQL on the backend.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="architecture">

A PHP backend over a MySQL database serves two sides: staff pages for book and stock management, and a client-facing site with a marketplace and ordering flow.

<Chapter id="decisions">

Separate staff and client experiences so each stays simple for its users.

Keep stock and orders in one consistent database model, so an order immediately updates availability.

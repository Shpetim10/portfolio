<Chapter id="problem">

Local businesses rarely have a loyalty program that is easy to run. Paper stamp cards get lost, and large commercial platforms are expensive and complex. Ritema gives a small business a simple way to reward returning customers, and gives customers one app to collect points and redeem rewards.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="constraints">

A solo project with a fixed graduation deadline, covering backend, mobile app, database and full written documentation. Security mattered from the start, because points and coupons have real value.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="architecture">

A Spring Boot API secured with Spring Security and JWT manages businesses, customers, points, rewards and coupons. MySQL stores the core records and Redis caches frequently read data. A Flutter mobile app lets customers collect points and redeem rewards by scanning QR codes.

<Chapter id="decisions">

Stateless JWT authentication with Spring Security, so every request is verified and the API can scale without server-side sessions.

QR codes for collecting and redeeming points instead of manual codes, which keeps the interaction at the counter fast and simple.

Redis as a cache in front of MySQL for frequently read data, to keep the app responsive.

Core features first, extras later. With one developer and a fixed deadline, I protected the complete earn-and-redeem flow and scheduled smaller features after it.

<Chapter id="results">

Delivered a complete, working product and its full documentation on time for graduation, built entirely by me across backend, mobile app and database.

The screens below are the customer app, from sign-in to the wallet and profile. Customers earn Beats at each place and build a standing there, from First Beat to Known by Name. Across every place they build an access status, from Active to Crown, which the membership card shows beside a QR code. Rewards arrive as coupons and limited-time Drops that are claimed in the app and shown at the counter, and every Beat earned or spent stays on record in the wallet's ledger. Swipe a strip, or use its arrows.
